About Me

Your friend in Sri Lanka

Ayubowan! I'm Sameera — a professional tour guide and tour leader born and raised in Kegalle, right between Colombo and the hill capital Kandy. With over 100 tours guided across the island, I've helped families, couples and solo travelers from all over the world experience Sri Lanka with energy, laughter and a local's eye for hidden gems.

  • ✓Energetic and friendly — travelers say goodbye as friends, not clients
  • ✓Experienced tour leader with Ceylon Expeditions Travels
  • ✓Great with families and kids — patient, kind and fun
  • ✓Island-wide knowledge — culture, wildlife, beaches and hill country

Tours & Experiences

Private & Family Tours
