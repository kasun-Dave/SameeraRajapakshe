01 — ABOUT ME

Your friend in Sri Lanka

Ayubowan! I'm Sameera, a professional tour guide and tour leader born and raised in Kegalle, right in the heart of Sri Lanka between Colombo and Kandy. With over 100 tours guided across the island, I've helped families, couples and solo travelers from around the world experience Sri Lanka the way it should be experienced — with energy, laughter and a local's eye for the hidden gems.

  • Energetic and friendly — travelers say goodbye as friends, not clients
  • Experienced tour leader with Ceylon Expeditions Travels
  • Great with families and kids — patient, kind and fun
  • Island-wide knowledge: culture, wildlife, beaches, hill country
